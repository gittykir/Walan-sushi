Our Philosophy

Sushi as an Art Form

At Walan Sushi, every roll is a composition, crafted with fresh fish, perfectly seasoned sushi rice, and ingredients chosen for quality, not compromise. We believe sushi should be both beautiful and bold.

Based in Nyali, Mombasa, we bring the precision and elegance of Japanese sushi culture to the Kenyan coast, with a warm, local heart. Whether you dine in, take away, or order delivery, every piece is made the same way: with care.
